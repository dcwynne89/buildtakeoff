Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 7, 2026

What We Collect

BuildTakeoff processes all calculations locally in your browser. We do not collect, store, or transmit any project data, measurements, or calculation results.

Optional Account & Data Storage

All Build tools work fully without an account. If you choose to sign in, we store:

  • Your email address and display name
  • Saved calculations and project data
  • Client contact information you enter

This data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore and associated with your account. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time by emailing buildpdf.help@gmail.com.

If you do not create an account, no data is stored. All calculations remain 100% client-side regardless of account status.

Third-Party Services

  • Netlify — hosting and CDN delivery; may collect anonymized access logs
  • Google AdSense — displays advertisements and may set cookies for ad personalization
  • Google Firebase — optional account authentication and data storage

Cookies

This site uses cookies set by Google AdSense to serve relevant advertisements. Google may use cookies to personalize ads based on your browsing activity. You can opt out at Google Ad Settings.

You can control cookie settings through your browser. Disabling cookies may affect the display of advertisements but will not affect calculator functionality.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please email us at buildpdf.help@gmail.com.
