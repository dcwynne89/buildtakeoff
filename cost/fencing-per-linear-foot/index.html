Fence Cost Breakdown

Materials

Fence materials typically account for 40-60% of the total installed cost. Here's a per-linear-foot material breakdown for a 6-foot wood privacy fence:

  • Posts (4×4 PT): $10 – $20 each, set every 6-8 feet = $1.50 – $3/LF
  • Rails (2×4 PT): 2-3 rails per bay = $2 – $4/LF
  • Pickets (1×6 cedar or PT): ~2 per foot = $4 – $8/LF
  • Concrete (post footings): 1-2 bags per post = $0.50 – $1.50/LF
  • Hardware (screws, brackets): $0.50 – $1/LF

Labor ($8 – $20 per linear foot)

Fence installation labor varies by material and terrain. Wood privacy fences run $10 – $15/LF for labor. Chain link is faster at $8 – $12/LF. Vinyl and ornamental fencing require more precision at $12 – $20/LF. Rocky or sloped terrain adds 20-40% to labor costs.

Posts — The Hidden Cost Driver

Post installation is the most labor-intensive part of fencing. Each post requires a hole 8 – 36 inches deep (depending on frost line) and 10-12 inches in diameter. Manual digging runs $15 – $25 per hole; power auger reduces this to $8 – $15. In rocky soil, expect $25 – $50+ per hole.

Gates

  • Walk gate (3-4 ft): $150 – $400 installed
  • Double drive gate (10-12 ft): $400 – $1,200 installed
  • Sliding gate (automated): $1,500 – $5,000 installed
  • Each gate requires heavier posts (4×6 or 6×6) and stronger hardware
