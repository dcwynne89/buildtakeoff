Roofing Cost Breakdown Per Square

A "roofing square" equals 100 square feet of roof area. Here's what makes up the total installed cost per square.

Materials

Material cost is the largest variable. A square of 3-tab shingles costs $90 – $120 at wholesale, while architectural shingles run $100 – $170. Metal panels range from $250 – $600 per square depending on gauge and profile. Tile can exceed $500 – $1,000 per square for premium clay.

Labor ($50 – $80 per square)

Basic shingle installation runs $50 – $65 per square on a walkable roof (6/12 pitch or less). Steep roofs (8/12+) add 20-40% to labor costs due to safety harnesses, staging, and slower production. Metal and tile installation is significantly more labor-intensive: $80 – $150+ per square.

Tear-Off ($100 – $150 per square)

Removing old shingles typically costs $100 – $150 per square. A second layer tear-off (removing two layers) runs $150 – $200 per square. Most codes allow a maximum of two layers before tear-off is mandatory.

Dump Fees ($15 – $50 per square)

Roofing debris disposal runs $15 – $50 per square depending on local landfill rates. A typical 25-square roof generates 4,000 – 6,000 lbs of debris. Budget for a 20-yard dumpster rental ($350 – $500) for most residential roofs.

Additional Costs

  • Underlayment: Felt ($5 – $10/sq) or synthetic ($15 – $25/sq)
  • Flashing: $5 – $15/linear foot at valleys, walls, and penetrations
  • Ridge Cap: $4 – $8/linear foot
  • Drip Edge: $2 – $5/linear foot
  • Pipe Boots & Vents: $15 – $60 each
  • Ice & Water Shield: $50 – $80/roll (required in cold climates at eaves)
