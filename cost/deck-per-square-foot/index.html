Deck Cost Breakdown

Decking Materials

The decking surface (boards) makes up 30-40% of total cost. Prices per square foot for boards only:

  • Pressure-treated pine (5/4×6): $3 – $5/sq ft
  • Cedar (5/4×6): $5 – $9/sq ft
  • Redwood: $8 – $14/sq ft
  • Composite (mid-range): $8 – $14/sq ft
  • Composite (premium/capped): $12 – $22/sq ft
  • PVC (Azek, TimberTech): $14 – $24/sq ft

Framing & Substructure

The framing — joists, beams, posts, and ledger board — accounts for 25-35% of total cost. Framing is almost always pressure-treated lumber regardless of decking material.

  • Joists (2×8 or 2×10 PT): $4 – $7/sq ft of deck area
  • Beam (doubled 2×10 or 2×12): $3 – $6/linear foot
  • Posts (6×6 PT): $15 – $30 each
  • Post footings (Sonotubes + concrete): $30 – $75 each
  • Ledger board attachment: $200 – $500 (lag bolts, flashing)

Hardware & Fasteners

  • Joist hangers: $3 – $6 each (1 per joist end)
  • Post bases/anchors: $15 – $40 each
  • Deck screws (stainless): $0.50 – $1/sq ft of decking
  • Hidden fasteners (composite): $1.50 – $3/sq ft
  • Bolts, flashing, misc: $50 – $150 total

Labor ($8 – $22 per sq ft)

Deck labor runs $8 – $15/sq ft for a simple ground-level PT wood deck. Elevated decks (3+ feet off ground) run $15 – $22/sq ft due to stairs, railing, and more complex framing. Composite installation costs roughly the same as wood for labor.

Permits ($100 – $500)

Most jurisdictions require a building permit for any attached deck or a deck more than 30" above grade. Permit fees range from $100 – $500 and typically require a site plan and structural details.
