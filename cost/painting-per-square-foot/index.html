What Goes Into Painting Cost?

Paint ($35 – $70 per gallon)

Paint is the most variable cost element. Here's how the grades break down:

  • Builder-grade (economy): $25 – $35/gallon. Covers ~350 sq ft/gal. Suitable for rentals and new construction base coats.
  • Mid-grade (standard): $35 – $50/gallon. Covers ~350 – 400 sq ft/gal. Best value for most residential repaints.
  • Premium (Sherwin-Williams, Benjamin Moore): $50 – $70/gallon. Better coverage, durability, and color accuracy. Covers ~400 sq ft/gal.
  • Specialty (exterior, cabinet, epoxy): $60 – $100+/gallon. Required for high-wear or specialized surfaces.

Labor ($25 – $50 per hour)

Painting labor runs $25 – $35/hour for general painters and $40 – $50/hour for experienced professionals. Most contractors bid by the square foot rather than hourly. A typical crew of 2-3 painters can complete 800 – 1,200 sq ft of wall area per day for interior work.

Prep Work

Prep is often the hidden cost driver. Properly prepared surfaces take more paint efficiently and last longer.

  • Cleaning / Pressure washing: $0.10 – $0.30/sq ft
  • Scraping & sanding: $0.50 – $1.50/sq ft (peeling surfaces)
  • Caulking: $1 – $3/linear foot
  • Priming: $0.50 – $1/sq ft (bare or stained surfaces)
  • Patching / Drywall repair: $2 – $8 per patch
  • Lead paint testing & encapsulation: $300 – $600+ (pre-1978 homes)

Factors That Affect Painting Cost

Surface Condition

New drywall with a simple prime-and-paint is fastest and cheapest. Peeling, chalking, or stained surfaces require extensive prep that can double the labor cost. Water-damaged areas may need drywall repair before painting can begin.

Number of Coats

Two coats is standard for repaints. Going from a dark to a light color may require 3 coats or a tinted primer + 2 coats. Each additional coat adds roughly 30-40% to labor time.

Accent Walls & Multi-Color

Every color change means brush cleanup, new masking, and potential waste. Budget $50 – $100 extra per accent wall or color change within a room.

Trim, Doors & Windows

  • Baseboards: $1 – $3/linear foot
  • Door frames: $25 – $75 each
  • Doors (both sides): $50 – $150 each
  • Window trim: $25 – $60 per window
  • Crown molding: $1 – $3/linear foot
