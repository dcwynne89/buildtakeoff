Why Every Contractor Needs a Unit Converter

Unit conversions are one of the most common sources of costly mistakes on construction projects. A simple error — confusing cubic feet with cubic yards, or square feet with roofing squares — can mean ordering 9× too much or too little material.

Common Conversion Mistakes

  • Cubic feet vs. cubic yards: 1 cubic yard = 27 cubic feet. Ordering "27" cubic yards when you meant cubic feet means 27× too much concrete.
  • Roofing squares vs. square feet: 1 square = 100 sq ft. A 20-square roof is 2,000 sq ft, not 20 sq ft.
  • Board feet vs. linear feet: Board footage accounts for width and thickness. A 2×12 at 8 feet is 16 board feet, not 8.
  • Tons vs. cubic yards: Gravel is sold by the ton but measured by the cubic yard. You need to know the material density to convert.
